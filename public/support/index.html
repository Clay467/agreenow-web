Support

Need help with AgreeNow? Here are some common questions and answers.

How do I create an agreement?

Tap the Create tab, select a template, fill in the fields, sign, and send to the other party.

How does the other party sign?

After you send an agreement, the recipient will receive a notification. If they have the app, it appears in their Sign tab. Otherwise, they can sign via a web link.

How do I export a completed agreement?

Open a completed agreement from the Home tab and tap the share icon in the top-right corner. This generates a PDF you can save, print, or share.

Is my signature legally binding?

Electronic signatures are legally recognized in most jurisdictions under laws like the ESIGN Act and UETA. However, AgreeNow does not provide legal advice. Consult an attorney if you have questions about enforceability.

Can I edit an agreement after it's signed?

No. Once both parties have signed, the agreement is locked and cannot be modified.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile and tap "Delete Account" at the bottom. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.
