Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number, and the content of agreements you create (including signatures). We also collect device information and usage data to improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the service (creating and delivering agreements), authenticate your identity, communicate with you about your account, and improve the App.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Agreement data and signatures are stored in secure cloud infrastructure with access controls limiting visibility to authorized participants only.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share agreement data only with the parties you designate as participants. We may share data with service providers who assist in operating the App (hosting, email delivery) under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data and agreements for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also request a copy of your data. Contact us at support@agreenow.app to exercise these rights.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the App or email.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at support@agreenow.app.
